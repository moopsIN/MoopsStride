# Stride by Moops: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Stride is a running tracker made by Moops Design ("we"). This policy explains what Stride collects, where it's kept, and how to delete it. In short: your runs stay on your phone unless you sign in, and we never sell your data or show ads.

## Using Stride without an account

Without an account, everything stays on your device: your runs (including the GPS route), your profile and your settings. We don't receive any of it. If you uninstall the app, it's gone.

## Using Stride with an account

If you sign in with Google or email, Stride backs up your data to your own private space in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so you can use it on other devices:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address and an account ID | To sign you in |
| Your runs: route (precise location), start and end time, distance, duration, pace, calories, steps, splits | To show your history and stats on every device |
| Your profile: goal, experience level, gender, age, height, weight, activity level, distance unit | To estimate calories and personalise the app |

Only you can access this data. Security rules allow each account to read and write only its own records.

## Location

Stride uses your precise location **only while you're recording a run**, including with the screen locked so a run isn't interrupted. It doesn't track you at any other time.

Map tiles are loaded from MapTiler, which means MapTiler sees the general area you're viewing and your IP address, like any website. Stride doesn't send them your runs or account.

## Crash reports and app integrity

- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a technical report: the error, your device model, OS version and an anonymous installation ID. It doesn't include your runs or profile.
- **App integrity.** Firebase App Check confirms requests come from the real Stride app, using Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest.

## What we don't do

- No ads and no advertising IDs.
- No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
- No analytics or tracking beyond the crash reports described above.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Delete a run:** delete it in the app. It's also removed from your account.
- **Sign out:** this removes your data from that device. Your account copy stays.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, runs and profile from our servers and from the device.
- You can also ask us to delete your data by email (below).

## Children

Stride isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
